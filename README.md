### 🌟 About Me

I am a **Computer Science & Engineering student** with a deep passion for **Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Data Storytelling**. I specialize in bridging the gap between raw data and strategic business decisions—transforming messy datasets into clean, actionable, and visually captivating insights.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Building end-to-end analytics pipelines, interactive Power BI dashboards, and predictive models.
- 💡 **Superpower:** Asking the right questions of data, spotting hidden anomalies, and designing clean visual narratives that executive teams can act on immediately.
- 🎯 **Career Goal:** Launching my career as a **Data Analyst / BI Analyst** where I can solve high-impact operational and business challenges.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** **SQL queries, complex joins, Pandas wrangling, DAX formulas, EDA techniques, and data visualization.**
